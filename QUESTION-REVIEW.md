## Selection behavior

- Fresh source facts take priority over alternative wording of an already seen fact.
- History persists between runs and includes questions displayed but not yet answered.
- Question order, answer order, and section starting order vary independently.
- A normal session includes at most 20% due review when fresh facts remain. Missed & due practice is a separate choice.
- Boss encounters use the same history and avoid the mission’s questions when the pool permits.
- A deliberate missed-answer retry is labeled and follows at least two other facts. It is not an accidental repeat.
- A small exhausted pool reuses the least-recently displayed questions; a session never duplicates an ID except a labeled retry.
- Full-set Study retains every eligible question, including alternative forms, with source facts spread through the set.

Question-level review dates supplement existing concept progress. Older records and retired question IDs remain readable as history. Older saved sessions refresh their remaining questions while preserving attempts and combat state. Export/import includes presentation history; reset learning clears it.
