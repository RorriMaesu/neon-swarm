# Section coverage — Bodyguard 2.1

15,976 source-linked questions across all 169 numbered teaching sections and 28 chapter introductions. The map includes 606 textbook learning objectives and 1,724 questions from table cells. There are also 52 authored questions connecting mechanisms, examples, and career discussions.

## How the bank works

Terminology questions use public chapter definitions. Relationship and applied-recall questions complete short source statements, with the correct statement explained after answering. Table questions ask about a named structure, factor, action, attachment, or other listed relationship. Authored questions address important mechanisms and topics that a purely mechanical extraction could miss. Original pathway and diagram encounters remain as additional practice; they are not included in the expansion count.

The bank removes vague blanks, unresolved pronouns, image-dependent prompts, and flattened table prose. Close aliases such as molecule/compound and anterior/ventral are excluded where they could make a source-completion question ambiguous. Duplicate phrasing is counted once within a chapter. Questions and answer order are shuffled in play.

## What the coverage audit establishes

Every numbered section and introduction has questions. Every named teaching heading with explanatory text is represented; empty parent headings introduce their child topics. Textbook objectives are listed in the section browser. Video-only interactive activities are linked through the textbook rather than converted into invented questions.

This is a section and topic coverage audit. It does not certify that every individual sentence, diagram detail, numerical value, or possible interpretation has been exhaustively assessed. The bank is a substantial source-aligned practice resource and still needs independent instructor review for item quality, ambiguity, and pedagogical depth. The glossary and passage-completion questions emphasize recall; they do not replace extended explanation or clinical reasoning.
