# Curriculum coverage and authoring

Aligned edition: OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e. The game contains eight selected core concepts per chapter and three prompt forms per concept: identification, defining relationship, and a scenario. There are 726 variants including the pathway and scanner variants. Pathways occur in 16 chapters; scanners are original heart and nephron-direction schematics.

This is a starting practice bank, not complete textbook coverage. The concepts and explanations were author-checked against public chapter references; no independent instructor review or measured learning-efficacy study has been completed. The bank avoids textbook artwork and instructor assessments.

## Content format

curriculum.js stores a term, its defining relationship, a direct question, and an application scenario for each core concept. learning.js prepares answer targets from related concepts and preserves stable answer identities while shuffling presentation. Connections questions ask for the best defining description, avoiding a claim that a subtype description is the broadest definition.

The first four concepts form Structure & identity and the last four form Function & regulation. These are broad practice groups, not a replacement for the book’s section structure. Source links go to the chapter review, which links onward to detailed sections.

Current concept IDs use chapter and row number. Preserve existing row order when editing or extending the bank; append new concepts or explicitly migrate IDs if reorganizing. Update the content version when changing meaning or evaluation. Review each prompt for a single best answer, plausible distractors, a useful explanation, and alignment with its selected chapter.

Pathways have explicit ordered steps and explanations; label simplified routes rather than implying they contain every intermediate structure. Diagram questions have equivalent text descriptions. Feedback gauges are limited to scenarios where a regulatory response can plausibly move a deviation toward a target.

Check new content with the tests, then obtain A&P instructor review for broader classroom use. Factual validation, accessibility checks, and automated structural checks serve different purposes.
