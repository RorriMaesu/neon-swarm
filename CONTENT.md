# Content and authoring

Bodyguard 2.3 includes 10,296 source-linked questions across all 169 numbered teaching sections and 28 introductions. It preserves 606 textbook learning objectives as goals.

The bank contains public glossary definitions, standalone source-completion recall, table relationships, and 153 authored questions (52 prior supplements and 101 new replacements). The original heart/nephron schematics and pathway activities are additional encounters. See COVERAGE.md for section counts and QUESTION-REVIEW.md for quality checks, corrections, selection behavior, and review limits. question-review.json records every original question’s disposition; coverage.json contains the current scope map.

Questions are static and load per chapter. learning.js selects varied sessions using persistent question and source-fact history, and validates compact saved decks and learning progress. Retired IDs are valid history but are excluded from play. Older active sessions are refreshed on resume.

All learning material is adapted or authored against public OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology 2e content, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Every question links to its section; definition items also link to the chapter glossary. No restricted instructor material, textbook artwork, or live question service is used. Attribution is in LICENSE.md.
