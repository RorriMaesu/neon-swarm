# Content and authoring

Bodyguard 2.1 expands the original eight-concept chapter sampler into **15,976 section-linked questions**. All 169 numbered sections and 28 introductions have practice. The section map includes all 606 public learning objectives and named teaching headings.

The bank contains public glossary-based terminology, source-completion recall, examples and applied passages, 1,724 table questions, and 52 authored questions for important mechanisms and topic gaps. The original procedural heart/nephron schematics and 16 pathway activities are additional encounters.

Choose a chapter, a section or Whole chapter, and All types or a particular question type. Study offers 20, 40, or the full question set. A chapter mission is a short sample; repeated runs prioritize unseen questions. Learning progress tracks distinct questions and topics within the selected scope.

See COVERAGE.md for section counts, the distinction between topic coverage and exhaustive factual assessment, and remaining instructor-review limits. coverage.json contains the machine-readable audit. Section objectives and topic lists are available in the game through Explore section coverage.

## Files

- curriculum.js preserves the original authored concepts and pathways.
- section-catalog.js lists sections, learning objectives, topic IDs, and counts.
- content/chapter-01.js through chapter-28.js contain static question banks. The selected chapter loads on demand, including from the complete local folder.
- learning.js handles selection, scope, shuffling, compact checkpoints, review priority, and progress validation.

## Reuse

Short source statements and definitions are adapted from public OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e content, licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Attribution appears in each learning view and the page footer. Every question links to its section, and definition questions also link to the public glossary. No live generator or external question service is used.

The bank has automated integrity checks and targeted author review; no independent A&P instructor review or learning-outcome study has been completed.
